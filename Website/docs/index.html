Analysis of Team Performance and Goals Scored

STAT 330 · Project checkpoint

Our research question

How well can we predict the number of goals scored by a soccer team in a match using other measurable match events? Specifically, we will consider shots, completed passes, corner kicks, fouls committed, and completed dribbles.

Group members

  • Kegley Terry
  • McKay Chandler
  • Ben Kirkham

Data source

We use StatsBomb Open Data from matches at the 2022 FIFA World Cup. Our observational unit is one team in one match. The data record professional soccer events, which we will aggregate into team-level match statistics.

Source: StatsBomb — media pack and open-data information

What we are studying

Our response variable is the number of goals scored by a team in a match. We plan to study associations with five measurable match statistics and use statistical models to assess predictive performance.

This is an observational study. Associations do not establish that a particular match event causes goals.
